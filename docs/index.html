The Check-in Flow

A quiet ritual. Three focused steps.

Each check-in takes 3–5 minutes. The flow is designed to feel like turning pages, not tapping a feed.

01

Where do you feel it?
Choose body areas. Then choose their sensations. Rate intensity 1–10. Before you name the emotion — you locate it.

AI Reflection

Not therapeutic reassurance — something to go deeper with.

The AI reflection card is designed to feel like reading a thoughtful note from a careful observer, not a chatbot response. It connects your physical signals to the named emotions, surfaces patterns, and offers a micro-exercise when relevant.

Set in Instrument Serif. Because reflection should feel like journal, not interface.
