# The body scan has no right answer.

Most apps ask: how are you feeling on a scale of 1 to 10? But before you can answer that, your shoulders have already told a different story. Your chest, your throat, your gut — they register things before your words do.

Cathier's body scan doesn't ask you to diagnose what's wrong. It asks you to listen to where your body is right now.

24 checkpoints — throat, chest, shoulders, lower back, hands, gut. You pick what you notice today.

No grade. No analysis of what it means. Just the act of noticing.

That noticing — that small moment of honest listening — is itself the practice.

Osho put it simply: awareness isn't a tool to fix what's there. Awareness is the change.

You don't have to process everything you notice. Seeing it is enough for today.
