# Listening, not fixing.

The body scan has no right answer. Only what your throat says today. What your chest says today.

Most tools built for emotional health ask you to diagnose yourself — 'How stressed are you, 1 to 10?' But stress isn't one thing. It lives in different places and says different things depending on the day.

Cathier's body scan gives you 24 checkpoints: throat, chest, gut, shoulders, lower back, hands — plus seven traditional energy centers for those who use that language.

You don't pick the 'right' one. You pick what you actually notice today.

There's no feedback that says 'this means you're anxious' or 'this means something is wrong.'

Just: what did you hear?

Osho described it this way — witnessing isn't a method to change what's there. It is the change.

You don't have to process everything you notice. Seeing it is enough for today.
