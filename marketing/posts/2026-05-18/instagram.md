Pattern Insights doesn't unlock until you've checked in 7 times. That's a design choice.

Most apps deliver "insights" within 24 hours of signup — before any real pattern could exist. That's not insight. It's an incentive to keep opening the app.

Cathier's Pattern Insights only activates after 7 check-ins. And it gets genuinely interesting after 30.

Here's the reasoning: one check-in tells you what you felt once. Seven check-ins begin to show where emotions cluster, which body signals repeat, what triggers you haven't yet connected to a word. The data needs room to accumulate before anything it says is worth trusting.

When it unlocks, it opens into four focus modes:

Stress triggers — what keeps igniting the same response?
Growth — where are you showing more steadiness than before?
Body signals — shoulders, throat, chest: which keeps showing up?
Open exploration — for what doesn't fit a clean category yet.

The goal isn't to optimize your emotions. It's to accumulate enough honest moments that something real — a pattern worth reflecting on — finally has enough to emerge from.

Seven is a floor. Not a ceiling.
