Here's a design decision that might seem unusual at first: Cathier locks its pattern analysis feature until you've logged at least 7 check-ins.

Most apps offer "insights" almost immediately — sometimes within the first day of signing up. It feels encouraging. But a pattern built from two or three entries isn't really a pattern yet. It's a guess dressed up as data.

The feature we call Pattern Insights takes a different approach. It stays quiet through your first six check-ins, and opens on the seventh. After 30 check-ins, it becomes genuinely useful — showing you which emotions tend to cluster on certain days, which parts of the body keep signaling tension, and what kinds of events tend to trigger the same responses.

It organizes what it finds into four areas: stress triggers, signs of growth, recurring body signals, and open exploration for things that don't fit a neat category.

There's no shortcut to building up your own honest data. But the patterns, once they have enough to work with, tend to say something worth sitting with.
