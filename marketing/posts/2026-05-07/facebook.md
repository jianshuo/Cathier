# Body first

There's something most emotion apps have in common: they all start by asking "how are you feeling today?"

But that's the second step, not the first.

Before you can name a feeling, your body has already registered it. The tightness in your chest when something is wrong. The heaviness in your shoulders when you're carrying more than you realized. The hollow feeling in your stomach when something important is missing.

Cathier begins somewhere different — with a body scan. You choose where in your body you feel something right now: throat, chest, stomach, shoulders, back. Then you select from a set of 65 physical sensations — not just "tense" or "heavy," but words like "constricted," "hollow," "pulsing," "burning."

From there, you name the emotion.

This order matters. When you start with the body, you tend to be more honest with yourself. It is harder to say "I'm fine" when your chest is telling a different story.

Cathier's check-in takes about 30 seconds. We built it this way because small, consistent moments of noticing add up — more than any extended journaling session once a week.

What does your body have to say today?
