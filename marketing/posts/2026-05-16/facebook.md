There's a gap between "I'm fine" and "I'm not fine." Most emotion apps ignore it. Cathier was built for it.

The Awareness Dictionary inside Cathier maps 79 emotions across 9 categories — not as a clinical reference, but as a practical field guide. Something you pull out in the middle of a moment you can't yet name.

Three ways to use it:

If you know the word: Search "forlorn" and see which body parts carry it, what sensations come with it.

If you feel something physical: Start from "shoulders tense" and find which emotions most often live there.

If the feeling is vague: Browse the 10 words in the joy category — elation, contentment, aliveness — and find where today actually sits.

Precision isn't just for academics. Naming what you feel accurately is one of the most useful things you can do with a hard moment.

Search "Cathier" on the App Store.
