79 emotions. 65 body sensations. 24 body parts. This isn't a mood tracker — it's a field guide.

Most emotion apps hand you a menu: happy, sad, anxious. Three words for the full range of being human. We thought that was worth expanding.

Cathier's Awareness Dictionary has three ways in:

→ Know the word: Search "forlorn" — find which body parts carry it, what sensations accompany it, and what it asks of you next.

→ Feel the sensation: Start from "chest tightness" — trace which emotions live there.

→ Have a vague sense: Browse the joy category and find whether today felt more like elation, contentment, or aliveness.

A field guide isn't read cover to cover. It's pulled out in the middle of something you can't yet name.

That's what we built.
