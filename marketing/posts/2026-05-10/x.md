# Osho: joy and happiness aren't the same thing.

Osho: joy and happiness aren't the same. Happiness needs an event; joy doesn't. Cathier has 10 words for joy. 雀跃: body-lightness before the mind catches up. 沛然: inner fullness in motion. 畅然: clear passage. "happy" holds none of them.
