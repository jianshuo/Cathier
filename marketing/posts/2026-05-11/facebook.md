# Anger doesn't have to become resentment.

Anger doesn't have to become resentment.

Osho made a distinction that stayed with me: suppressed anger turns into resentment — it waits, hardens. Witnessed anger becomes awareness. The difference isn't willpower. It's whether you can locate the feeling before it moves somewhere else.

Most of us learned to push anger down. What Cathier teaches instead: ask where it lives first.

Is it in your chest — a tight, heavy feeling that's closer to hurt? In your throat — something that hasn't been spoken? In your hands — a tension that wants movement but has nowhere to go?

Three different places. Three different messages.

Cathier has 8 words for anger in its awareness dictionary — not to overwhelm you, but because "I'm angry" leaves most of the feeling unnamed. The naming is the beginning of the witnessing.

And the witnessing is where anger stops becoming resentment.
