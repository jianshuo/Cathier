# The gap between stimulus and response

Most emotion apps ask: "How do you feel?"

Cathier asks first: "What happened?"

That optional field holds the space Osho called the gap — the one second between something landing on you and your reaction forming. Frankl said freedom lives there.

We stop there. #Cathier #somatics
