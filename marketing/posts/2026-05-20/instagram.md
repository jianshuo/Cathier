Your body scan doesn't just end at 'scan complete.' It decides what comes next.

After you finish a Cathier check-in, the AI doesn't push a generic 'try some breathing.' It reads the specific body parts and sensations you logged — then suggests a micro-exercise matched to exactly that.

Logged tightness in your throat? You get three voiced exhales, aimed at releasing constriction in that area.
Logged heaviness in your shoulders? Shoulder-specific release work.
Logged chest contraction paired with the emotion 渺茫 (a quiet sense of drifting away)? A 4-step chest-opening practice, grounded in both the sensation and what you named.

This is the gap Cathier is trying to close: the space between 'I just noticed something in my body' and 'now what?'

The micro-exercise is the answer to that second question. Two minutes. No equipment. No change of location needed.

The body scan you just did is the input. The practice is its response.
