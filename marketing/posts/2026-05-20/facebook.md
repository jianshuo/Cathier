Your body scan knows something.

When you finish a check-in in Cathier, the app reads what you just reported — which body part, which sensation — and offers a short two-minute practice matched to that specific combination.

Not the same breathing exercise every time. Something chosen for what your body just said.

Noticed tension in your throat? You'll receive a breathing practice designed for that particular area.
Felt heaviness in your shoulders? A shoulder-release sequence, not a general relaxation prompt.
Felt tightness in your chest alongside a feeling you couldn't quite name? The practice addresses both.

It's a small thing. Two minutes, no special equipment, no change of location. But it's the difference between an app that notices what you shared and one that treats every check-in the same.
